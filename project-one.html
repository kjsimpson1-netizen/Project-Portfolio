Autonomous Robot

Overview

I worked together with 3 other Engineering Physics students to design an autonomous robot. The given challenge was to create a robot that can autonomously navigate a surface while following a line of electrical tape on the ground, that can also complete a series of tasks along the way. The specifics of of the tasks are located here.

Project Details

  • Main Role: Mechanical Designer and Manufacturer
  • Tools Used: Onshape, Lathe, Laser Cutter, Water Jet, 3D Printer
